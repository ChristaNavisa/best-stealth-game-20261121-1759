Bellwether

Five heists by lamplight. One museum, five nights — steal a different treasure from a different wing each time, then reach the green EXIT unseen. Finish a heist without ever being spotted for a GHOST rating.

WASD / arrows move  ·  Shift run (fast, loud)  ·  C sneak (slow, silent)  ·  Click throw a pebble to lure a guard  ·  Q throw a smoke veil  ·  X / right-click toss a brass coin lure  ·  P pause  ·  R restart

Click a heist, or press 1–5, to begin.

How to stay unseen: guards see you inside their yellow cones — faster in lamplight, slower while you sneak or hide in the potted plants. A guard who half-sees you grows suspicious and comes to look; one who sees you clearly gives chase. Break sight, hide, and the alarm fades — the alarm level (0–3) on the meter shows how hot you are. Running is loud: guards hear it and come to investigate. Security cameras sweep red cones: linger in one and it trips a lockdown that sends every guard hunting at once. Laser tripwires blink on and off — cross a doorway only while the beam is dark. Toss a brass coin (X or right-click) to pull a guard away; pebbles do the same, quieter and shorter. Throw a smoke veil (Q, 2 per heist) where a cone cannot be dodged — guards and cameras cannot see through smoke, or see you inside it. Five heists, five treasures: the Bell, the Tide Idol, the Gilded Manuscript, the Brass Astrolabe — and the Grand Haul, which demands the Bell and the Idol in one night. Finish unseen for a GHOST rating.
